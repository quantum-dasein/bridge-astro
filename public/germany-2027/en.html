Programme topics

  1. Topic 01

    Planning and calculation methods for construction projects

    The proposed meeting with GP Verkehrswegebau on 21 April covers estimating methods for roadworks. How do planners account for crew size and equipment output when planning a shift?

  2. Topic 02

    Urban and municipal planning

    How can a street accommodate the road, utilities and drainage? In Halle, the proposed discussion covers who approves the layout and how access to buildings is maintained.

  3. Topic 03

    Construction project management

    An active construction site provides a setting to discuss how a contractor sequences the work. A bridge or another civil engineering project is proposed for the visit on 22 April.

  4. Topic 04

    Production management and quality management

    The Ammendorf visit on 20 April covers concrete and asphalt production. On 23 April, the proposed BGI Brambach visit focuses on how laboratory staff test materials and interpret the results.

  5. Topic 05

    Effective waste management

    The proposed visit to the Roitzsch landfill covers the acceptance and recording of construction waste. How do staff identify the contents of a load and record its acceptance?

  6. Topic 06

    Digitalisation (drones)

    20 April · Ammendorf · morning visit

    The proposed Ammendorf visit covers drone surveys of construction sites and the measurement of material volumes. How can survey images be used to calculate a stockpile’s volume and check the result?

  7. Topic 07

    Human resource management, training and development

    Dinner with Uzbek apprentices offers an opportunity to ask how workplace training is organised and what tasks they take on.

  8. Topic 08

    Operation and management of built assets

    The proposed Halle-Diemitz meeting covers road maintenance. How does the maintenance team record a defect, assign a crew and check the repair?

  9. Topic 09

    The role of government in the quality of German roads

    The proposed meeting with Die Autobahn GmbH des Bundes covers federal motorway management. How are responsibilities shared when planning and operating a road?

  10. Topic 10

    Meeting participants in Germany’s construction market

    Proposed meetings bring together materials producers, GP contractors, BGI Brambach and road authorities. With each organisation, discuss its role at a particular stage of a project.

20–23 APRIL · HALLE AND THE REGION

Organisations and sites

These are proposed meetings. The hosts will confirm access, speakers and sites ahead of the visit. Use the official resources below to prepare for each discussion. The questions below are suggested discussion points. Specific examples and demonstrations are subject to agreement with the hosts.

GP Günter Papenburg AG

The proposed host partner grew from the haulage business Günter Papenburg founded in 1963. The itinerary brings together group businesses working in materials production, infrastructure construction and waste management.

Group history ↗

20 and 23 April

Materials and testing

GP Betonwerke Ost

The Halle-Ammendorf ready-mixed concrete plant is part of the GP production network.

Follow a batch of pavement concrete from aggregate moisture correction to dispatch. Can the delivery ticket be traced to the test results and placement location?

Plant locations ↗

BGI Brambach GmbH

This Halle laboratory tests materials for road construction, earthworks and foundations.

Review a sample test report. Who takes and labels the specimen, who checks the result, and what happens if it differs from the plant laboratory’s findings?

Laboratory profile ↗

20–22 April

Construction sites

GP Verkehrswegebau GmbH

This Halle-based contractor builds transport infrastructure. The programme proposes a visit to an active road project.

Compare the planned quantity, crew allocation and daily record for one shift. Which measurements substantiate the work completed?

GP road construction ↗

GP Spezialtiefbau GmbH

This specialist geotechnical contractor undertakes projects and supplies specialist equipment.

Explore how ground investigation informs the choice of construction method. Using a site example, discuss which movements are monitored and when readings trigger a change in the works.

Company and equipment ↗

21–22 April

City planning and road operations

Die Autobahn GmbH des Bundes · Niederlassung Ost

The eastern division is based in Halle. Its remit covers project preparation, construction supervision and motorway operations across the region.

Trace a defect from handover inspection to rectification. Who decides whether the repair is acceptable, and what records reach the maintenance team?

Eastern division ↗

Stadt Halle (Saale)

The municipal department for urban development and planning coordinates city planning in Halle.

Use one street project to examine how utilities, drainage, traffic and access to properties are coordinated before construction starts.

City planning department ↗

Straßenmeisterei Diemitz

The Diemitz road maintenance depot is part of the southern regional division of Saxony-Anhalt’s road authority.

Which defects call for an immediate response? Compare an inspection log, a maintenance crew’s work order and its completion record.

Regional road authority ↗

HASTRA-Service GmbH

This Halle company provides road construction, temporary traffic management, road marking and winter maintenance.

Examine a traffic management plan at a construction phase change. How are barriers and signs moved while maintaining pedestrian routes and equipment access?

HASTRA services ↗

20 April

Waste management

GP Papenburg Entsorgung Ost GmbH

The operator of the Class II landfill in Roitzsch accepts specified categories of waste against published criteria.

Trace a load from documentation and weighing to the acceptance decision. Discuss which materials could instead be sent for recycling and how to select a suitable recycling method.
