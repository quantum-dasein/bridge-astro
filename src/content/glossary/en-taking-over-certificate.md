A Taking-Over Certificate is the document by which the Engineer confirms that the Works (or a Section) have been completed in accordance with the contract and taken over by the Employer. In the FIDIC 2017 edition taking over is governed by Sub-Clause 10.1. It is one of the key dates of the contract: the defects period, retention and delay damages all depend on it.

> **In brief:** Under Sub-Clause 10.1 of FIDIC 2017 the Contractor applies for the Taking-Over Certificate not more than 14 days before the Works will be complete, and within 28 days the Engineer issues the certificate or rejects the application with reasons. The Defects Notification Period starts on the taking-over date and the first half of the retention is released.

## The taking-over procedure under Sub-Clause 10.1

1. The Contractor completes the Works and passes the Tests on Completion.
2. The Contractor gives a Notice applying for the certificate, not more than 14 days before the expected completion.
3. Within 28 days the Engineer issues the certificate stating the completion date, or rejects the application and states what remains to be done.
4. Minor outstanding work that does not affect use of the Works does not prevent taking over: it is listed.

<aside class="bc-note"><strong>Look at your own contract first</strong><p>The time limits and procedure here follow the FIDIC 2017 General Conditions. The Contract Data and Particular Conditions may change them, so check the text of your own contract.</p></aside>

## What changes after taking over

- The Defects Notification Period starts (Clause 11).
- Delay Damages stop accruing.
- The first half of the Retention Money is released (Sub-Clause 14.9).
- Responsibility for care of the Works passes to the Employer.

## Frequently asked questions

### What if the Engineer does not respond to the application?

The period for a response under the General Conditions is 28 days. The consequences of the Engineer's silence depend on the text of the contract, so record the date of the application and check Sub-Clause 10.1 and the Particular Conditions.

### Does the Taking-Over Certificate mean the Contractor has performed all its obligations?

No. Only the Performance Certificate, issued after the defects period ends, confirms that.
