The FIDIC Yellow Book is the Conditions of Contract for Plant and Design-Build. The Contractor designs to the Employer's Requirements and is responsible for the Works being fit for their purpose.

> **In brief:** The Yellow Book is used where the Contractor designs: the Employer sets the requirements, the Contractor produces the design (Clause 5) and answers for the result. The price is normally a lump sum, the contract is administered by the Engineer, and Clause 12 deals with Tests after Completion. The 1999 and 2017 editions are in use.

## How to choose between the FIDIC books

Choosing a book means choosing who designs and who carries the risk. First decide whose design it is and how much the Employer needs a fixed price; only then pick the form. A detailed comparison is in our article [on choosing between the Red and Yellow Books](/news/en/fidic-red-vs-yellow-book/).

## Frequently asked questions

### Which edition applies?

The 1999 and 2017 editions are both in use; a reprint of the 2017 edition with amendments was issued in 2022. Your contract determines which one applies.

### Can the terms of the book be changed?

Yes, through the Particular Conditions. They take priority over the General Conditions, so read the contract starting with them and the Contract Data.
