The arbitrator profile of **Larisa Belousova**, founder and director of BRIDGE Consult LLC, has been published in the **GAR Arbitrator Research Tool (ART)**, the professional arbitrator database of **Global Arbitration Review**.

It marks another step in the development of her practice in international contracts and dispute resolution.

## Experience

For more than **15 years** Larisa Belousova has worked with FIDIC contracts and international infrastructure projects. Her experience combines hands-on project management, contract administration, preparing and assessing the parties' claims, mediation and arbitration.

## Focus in construction disputes

- allocation of contractual risk;
- delay analysis and extensions of time;
- valuation of variations and the financial consequences of breach.

These are issues where legal assessment requires a deep understanding of the technical and commercial side of project delivery.

## Arbitration practice

**Ten concluded proceedings** as sole arbitrator, chair and co-arbitrator. Publication of the profile in GAR ART presents this experience to the wider international professional community.

> I thank my colleagues for their trust and cooperation, and the GAR team for preparing the profile.
>
> — Larisa Belousova
