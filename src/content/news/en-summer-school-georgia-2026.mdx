From **12 to 20 August 2026**, Georgia hosts [Summer School Georgia 2026](/summer-school-georgia-2026/) along the **Batumi — Poti — Borjomi** route: an international programme on FIDIC contract management, claims, delay analysis and dispute resolution.

The organisers describe the format bluntly: minimum theory, maximum practice, no lectures for the sake of a certificate.

## What the programme includes

Summer School combines four components into a single learning path:

- **Three FIDIC Academy certified trainings** — Contract Management and Administration, Claims and Disputes, Delay Analysis.
- **A BRIDGE Consult online course** on effective planning and scheduling of construction projects; access is granted to participants during the training.
- **A technical visit** to the Port of Poti development project.
- **Hands-on case work** running through the entire programme.

## The key difference: one project instead of a list of topics

A conventional FIDIC course walks through contract clauses one by one. This programme is built around the **full life cycle of a single infrastructure project**, and the case moves through four stages:

1. **One project** — baseline conditions, contract structure, risk allocation.
2. **Event and notices** — participants themselves identify the event, the risks, notice deadlines and the evidence required.
3. **Claims and delays** — preparing claims, extension of time (EOT) requests and analysing the impact of events on the programme.
4. **Determination and dispute** — Engineer's determinations, dispute avoidance and resolution mechanisms.

Participants work from the perspective of different project actors — Employer, Engineer, Contractor, contract manager, planner, claims specialist and dispute resolution consultant. There are no traditional lectures: the core format is case analysis, group work and professional discussion.

At registration, participants may submit practical questions or situations from their own work — the organisers undertake to reflect the most relevant ones in the exercises.

## Technical visit: Port of Poti

The first-day field module covers the Port of Poti development project. On a live site, participants examine the key delivery challenges, planning and progress monitoring, risk allocation, administration of variations and the parties' claims — discussed from the Employer's, Contractor's and Engineer's positions.

## The experts

The programme is delivered by four international experts who have worked on projects ranging from several million to several billion US dollars — as FIDIC Engineers, advisers to the parties, DAAB/DB members, adjudicators and specialists in claims and delay analysis:

- **Nino Tsaturova** (Georgia) — FIDIC Certified Trainer, member of the FIDIC President's List of Adjudicators, international construction lawyer; named FIDIC Academy Best Trainer in 2023.
- **Irakli Khergiani** (Georgia) — FIDIC Board member, member of the FIDIC President's List of Adjudicators, President of ACEG.
- **Larisa Belousova** (Uzbekistan) — member of the FIDIC Ethics Committee, FCCE, FCCP, MCIArb, DRBF DB Practitioner; over 15 years advising transport, road and airport projects across Central Asia.
- **Sergii Vdovenko** (Ukraine) — FCCE, FCCP, FCCM, PhD, professional engineer; over 24 years delivering industrial and infrastructure projects.

## Certificates

On completing the in-person part, participants receive **three international FIDIC Academy certificates** — one for each certified training in the programme, issued in accordance with FIDIC Academy rules and procedures.

A BRIDGE Consult professional development certificate is additionally issued for the online course upon passing the final assessment, together with a set of training materials, cases and document templates.

The programme also builds a practical foundation for pursuing international **FIDIC Credentialing**.

## Who it is for

The programme is designed for professionals **already working** with FIDIC contracts who want to systematise that experience at a more advanced level: project managers, Employer representatives, contractors, consulting engineers, contract managers, claims and disputes specialists, planners and construction lawyers.

The organisers note specifically that they expect a comparable professional level across the group, so that the discussion stays substantive.

## What is included

- Early check-in with breakfast and 1 night at Alliance Palace Batumi
- 7 nights at Crowne Plaza Borjomi
- Breakfasts and transfers within Georgia
- 4 days of in-person training, coffee breaks and lunches on training days
- Technical visit to an infrastructure project and site visits
- Training materials and 3 international FIDIC certificates
