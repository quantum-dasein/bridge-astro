## From practice to theory

That is our principle: we start with a construction project problem, review the documents, compare the parties' positions and justify the decision. This is how knowledge gains a concrete application at work.

## Tarjima: AI lecture translation

Meet **Tarjima**, our AI product for translation from Russian into Uzbek.

The lecturer teaches in Russian while participants read a text translation in Uzbek as the lecture unfolds. For professional terminology, Tarjima includes a **FIDIC glossary**.

- the translation appears as the lecturer speaks;
- on your phone via a link or QR code, with no installation or sign-up;
- Latin or Cyrillic script, with the Russian original alongside.
