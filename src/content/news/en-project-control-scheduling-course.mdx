We are about to launch the online course **“Project Controls and Schedule Management”** in the BRIDGE CONSULT PROFESSIONAL SERIES.

How do you tell whether a project will finish on time? What will the delays cost? Which actions will put things right?

On the course we will turn contract events and construction data into a system for managing time, cost and change.

## What participants will learn

**26 academic hours** of practical training, during which participants will learn to:

- build a work breakdown structure and a programme;
- review and update the programme and forecast project completion;
- link progress of the works to costs, payments, changes and risks;
- assess time and cost variances;
- analyse delays and substantiate an extension of time;
- prepare a recovery programme and material for management decisions.

## Our approach: from practice to theory

Training is built on working through real project situations: we solve the problem, go through the methods it needs, and check how to apply them in practice.

## Live Uzbek translation
