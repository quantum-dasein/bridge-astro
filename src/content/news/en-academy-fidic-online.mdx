The BRIDGE Consult practical programme on **FIDIC 1999 and 2017** contract management is now available online. A dedicated programme page has been opened — [BRIDGE Consult Academy](https://www.bridgeconsult.uz/academy/en/).

The programme [was introduced earlier](/news/en/fidic-training-practical-issues/) as an in-person workshop for the public authorities and employers of Uzbekistan. The online format removes the main constraint of in-person delivery: participants from different regions and organisations no longer need to gather in one city for several days in a row.

## What the programme covers

18 modules — from contract administration to arbitration — and more than 40 practical cases. The programme is deliberately **not** structured as a clause-by-clause walkthrough of the conditions of contract. Its purpose is to address the real, recurring problems that arise in practice and to build the ability to resolve them **before** they turn into an extension of time claim, an additional cost claim, a Variation dispute or a formal dispute referred to the DAAB or arbitration.

Every practical issue is analysed across the same set of fields: the typical scenario on site, the time impact, the cost impact, the applicable sub-clauses of the 1999 and 2017 editions, common mistakes set out separately for the Employer, the Engineer and the Contractor, and a scenario for group work.

## How the programme is delivered

Sessions are delivered **live** in Russian, with questions and analysis of the participants' own situations. Every session is recorded, and the recording stays with the participant.

Slides, handouts and the FIDIC terminology glossary are prepared **in Russian and Uzbek**. Uzbek subtitles are produced for the recordings: the translation is reviewed by a contracts specialist rather than a general translator — in FIDIC, precision of terminology is the substance.

Modules and cases can be assembled around a specific project and the employer's own contract — the programme is also delivered in-house for a single organisation.

## Who it is for

Public authorities and state employers delivering infrastructure projects — including the Ministry of Construction, the Ministry of Transport, JSC Uzsuvtaminot and the State Expertise under the Ministry of Construction — as well as project employers, engineers, consultants and project managers.

The programme is delivered by Larisa Belousova — FIDIC Certified Consulting Engineer (FCCE) and FIDIC Certified Consulting Professional (FCCP), member of the FIDIC Integrity Management Committee, and an ADB Accredited Contract Management and Dispute Avoidance Specialist.
